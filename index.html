Founder profile

Calm is a professional advantage.

Tejaswini More brings together two disciplines built on awareness: financial-fraud analysis and Shotokan karate.

A banking fraud and risk professional and third-degree Shotokan black belt, Tejaswini has spent years studying patterns, assessing intent and making careful decisions under pressure. She began karate at a young age, earned her first black belt at 13 and went on to teach students of different ages and experience levels.

Mindful Defence is her platform for translating those experiences into practical tools for ethical leadership, risk awareness and professional resilience.

Business plan

A focused path to market.

Mindful Defence begins as a founder-led advisory and education business, building authority through useful content before expanding into paid programs and partnerships.

Primary clients

  • Banks, fintech companies and risk teams
  • Small businesses without dedicated fraud training
  • Universities and early-career professional programs
  • Managers working in high-pressure environments

Revenue model

  • Fixed-fee corporate workshops
  • Short advisory and process-review engagements
  • Individual and enterprise course licences
  • Speaking, partnerships and commissioned programs

Market position

  • Human-centred risk education—not technical auditing
  • Fraud awareness combined with disciplined action
  • Founder-led and grounded in professional experience
  • Accessible to specialist and general audiences

Go-to-market

  • Publish regular insight-led LinkedIn content
  • Run pilot workshops with professional groups
  • Convert proven material into a flagship digital course
  • Build institutional partnerships and client referrals

Development roadmap

Phase one

Establish authority

Brand identity, founder profile, regular content and one clear methodology.

Phase two

Validate demand

Pilot workshops, participant feedback, case studies and a refined offer.

Phase three

Build scale

Digital courses, repeatable corporate programs and selected partnerships.
